Folio is an open-source document reader for Android: no ads, no tracking, no internet access.

<b>Formats</b>
PDF, EPUB, MOBI, FB2, CBZ/CBR comics, XPS, SVG, TXT, HTML, images, plus DOCX, XLSX and PPTX.

<b>Comfortable reading</b>
• page-by-page (swipe) or continuous scroll;
• pinch-zoom exactly where you pinch; double-tap zoom;
• table of contents, text search, page thumbnails (auto-hide, can be pinned);
• light and dark themes, dynamic colors.

<b>Draw and write on any document</b>
• pressure-sensitive pen, marker, underline, eraser (including partial erase);
• works on scans too — no text layer required;
• sticky notes: long-press exactly where you want one;
• undo and redo.

<b>Text tools</b>
• highlight, underline and copy by word, sentence or free range;
• long-press a word to highlight it;
• one list of all notes and highlights in the document.

<b>PDF editing</b>
• rotate, delete and insert pages, insert pages from another PDF;
• fill in forms;
• export a PDF with standard annotations that open in any viewer.

<b>Library</b>
Recent and full library, covers from the first page, filters, sorting, search, favorites, reading progress.

<b>Open source</b>
AGPL-3.0. Document engine: MuPDF. The app collects no data and has no internet permission.
